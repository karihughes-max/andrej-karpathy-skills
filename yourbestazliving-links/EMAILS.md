Hi {first name},

Since you mentioned you're relocating, here's what I've learned from helping people move here:

- **Visit in two seasons if you can.** Arizona in March and Arizona in July are different places.
- **Think about your daily drive** to work, the airport or family before you fall for a floor plan.
- **Make the most of a short visit.** When clients fly in, I plan a full day: a few homes, the neighborhoods around them, and somewhere good for lunch. You leave knowing the area, not just one house.

If it would help, I'm happy to do a 30-minute video call to talk through areas and timing. No pressure, just a plan.
